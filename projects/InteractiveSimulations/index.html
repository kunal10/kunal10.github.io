Interactive Simulations

Interactive visualizations and simulations in the Gemini app

Overview

Interactive Simulations brings interactive visualizations and simulations to the Gemini app. Moving beyond static text and images, Gemini turns a question into a hands-on model that users can explore, from physics simulations to complex 3D molecular structures. Users adjust parameters in real time, which helps them see not just the “what” but the “how” and “why” of a concept.

It launched on April 9, 2026 and rolled out globally to Gemini app users over the next few weeks. Asking Gemini to “show me” or “help me visualize” a topic with the Pro model is enough to try it, for example the double-slit experiment, a double pendulum, how fractals work, or a lunar orbit where velocity and gravity can be tuned.

This was a cross-functional effort across several teams at Google.
